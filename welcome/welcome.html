Steady Volume

One last step

Your browser asks before an extension can work on websites. Steady Volume only listens to the sound of videos and never reads or sends anything.

You’re all set

Play any film or programme, on any website. Whispers come up, explosions come down. There’s nothing else to do.

Good to know

  • Want it stronger or gentler? Click the Steady Volume icon in your toolbar. If you can’t see it, click the puzzle-piece icon and pin it.
  • Already had a video open? Reload that page once.
  • Want the film exactly as mixed? Click the icon and switch it Off. Switch it back On any time.
