What it does

The same tricks TV broadcasters use, working quietly in the background.

  1. 1

    Lifts the voices

    A gentle boost where speech lives, so consonants come through clearly. That’s the part of speech ears lose first.

  2. 2

    Evens out scenes

    Quiet scenes are raised slowly and loud scenes brought down, so you set the volume once.

  3. 3

    Catches the jolts

    A hard ceiling stops sudden bangs and jump-scares in milliseconds. Nothing gets past it.

Questions

Does it change the film?

Only how loud each moment is. Nothing is cut, and the music and effects are all still there. You can switch it off at any time to hear the original mix.

Will it help with hearing loss?

Many people with mild hearing loss find dialogue much easier to follow, because voices are lifted and loud sounds no longer force the volume down. It isn’t a medical device and doesn’t replace hearing aids.

Does the extension read what I watch?

No. It only adjusts the volume of sound as it plays, on your own computer. It doesn’t record, store or send anything anywhere.

Why not just use my TV’s setting?

Please do, and our guide above shows you where it is. Built-in settings are often designed for loud adverts rather than loud scenes, and vary a lot between brands. The Steady Box is tuned specifically for films.
